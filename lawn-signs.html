Show Your Support!

Show your support for Measure N by requesting a lawn sign! Signs will be ready for distribution in September, at which point a volunteer will be in touch to let you know our community pick-up locations.

How it works

Three steps to a sign on your lawn

  • 1
    Request your sign. Fill out the short form with your name and neighborhood.
  • 2
    We'll reach out. A volunteer will contact you once signs are ready in September.
  • 3
    Pick up & display. Grab your sign from a community pick-up location and show your support!
