Our district

Serving Terra Linda, Marinwood & Lucas Valley

Miller Creek School District (MCSD) provides an excellent education to elementary and middle school students in Terra Linda, Marinwood, and Lucas Valley. Our great schools protect both our quality of life and our property values.

The problem

Our schools are aging — and it shows

With most MCSD schools built 60 to 70 years ago, aging classrooms need repairs and updates to continue supporting quality education and meet current academic and safety standards.

Aging school infrastructure — including bathrooms, plumbing, roofs, electrical, heating, and cooling systems — is in need of repair. With growing wildfire risk, upgrades are needed to keep schools safe and functioning during smoky days and power outages.

The solution

Locally controlled funding, matched by the state

Voting YES on N provides locally controlled funding to repair and upgrade our elementary and middle schools. Measure N also helps MCSD qualify for approximately $7 million in state matching funds that would otherwise go to other communities. Because the state does not provide long-term, dedicated funding for facility upgrades, local bonds like this one are essential.

Well-maintained classrooms that meet today's educational and safety requirements.

State match unlocked

Fiscal accountability

Strict protections ensure funds benefit local schools only

No state or federal takeover

No funds can be taken by the state or federal governments — ever.

No admin salaries

No funds may be used for administrators' salaries or benefits.

Independent oversight

An independent oversight committee, annual audits, and public spending disclosures.
