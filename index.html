Why it matters

Good schools are the heart of our community

With most MCSD schools built 60 to 70 years ago, aging classrooms need repairs and updates to continue supporting quality education and meet current academic and safety standards. Bathrooms, plumbing, roofs, electrical, heating, and cooling systems are in need of repair — and with growing wildfire risk, upgrades are needed to keep schools safe and functioning during smoky days and power outages.

Whether or not you have school-age children, good schools protect our property values and quality of life.

What Measure N funds

Voting Yes on N will:

Every dollar stays local and is dedicated to repairing and modernizing our neighborhood schools.

Fix aging infrastructure

Repair deteriorating bathrooms, plumbing, roofs, HVAC, and electrical systems across our campuses.

Upgrade classrooms

Modernize learning spaces for math, science, technology, reading, writing, arts, and music.

Meet current standards

Provide well-maintained classrooms that meet today's educational and safety requirements.

Improve accessibility

Ensure classrooms, playgrounds, and campuses are accessible for students with disabilities.

Boost school security

Improve locks, lighting, emergency communications, and cameras across every campus.

Unlock state matching funds

Qualify MCSD for approximately $7 million in state matching funds that would otherwise go elsewhere.

Fiscal accountability

Every dollar stays local — by law

  • No funds taken by the state or federal government.
  • No funds used for administrators' salaries or benefits.
  • Independent oversight committee, annual audits, and public spending disclosures.
