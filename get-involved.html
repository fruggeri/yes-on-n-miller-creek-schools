Join us to help pass Measure N!

The Yes on N campaign is a grassroots effort that relies on support from our local community. No District resources can be used to campaign for Measure N, so we need help from local parents, teachers, and community members like you!

Ways to help

Please take a moment to sign up and get involved today

  • 1
    Spread the word. Talk to neighbors, friends, and family about why Measure N matters.
  • 2
    Volunteer your time. Help with canvassing, phone banking, or community events.
  • 3
    Host a lawn sign. Show your support right on your street — request one here.
  • 4
    Endorse the campaign. Add your name publicly — endorse Measure N.
